# Hydra Carbon Battery Material Specification

## 1. Scope

This document defines the public material concept behind the Hydra Carbon
Battery program.

The scope is the material itself, not the internal method used to identify,
rank, or optimize it.

## 2. Public Material Identity

- Public ID: `hcbm-1`
- Public Name: `Hydra Carbon Battery Material Candidate`
- Material Family: `hybrid_redox_polymer_scaffold`
- Maturity: `simulation-only candidate`

## 3. Functional Intent

`hcbm-1` is intended to act as a CO2-capture active material that:

- preferentially binds CO2 over major non-target gases
- remains usable under bounded humidity exposure
- can be regenerated by an electrical swing
- operates without depending on high-temperature bulk regeneration as the
  primary release path
- survives repeated capture and release cycles well enough to justify further
  study

## 4. Public Structural Description

The public material concept is a hybrid scaffold that combines:

- a porous capture domain for gas access and selective uptake
- redox-active domains that support electrically driven binding-state changes
- a conductive support path or conductive network for electro-swing coupling
- humidity-management features intended to reduce water-driven collapse or pore
  blockage

This package does not publish a full synthesis recipe, exact precursor list, or
the internal search path that selected this concept.

## 5. Target Property Envelope

The public target profile for `hcbm-1` is:

- CO2-selective under bounded concentrated-stream conditions
- stable enough under moderate humidity to avoid immediate failure
- electrically switchable in a conservative operating window
- compatible with room-temperature-adjacent operation
- capable of repeated cycling without immediate catastrophic degradation
- realistic enough for future cartridge-style device integration

## 6. Intended Boundary Conditions

The current intended boundary is intentionally narrow:

- controlled concentrated streams, not universal feedstocks
- conservative electrical swing, not aggressive excitation
- simulation-first evaluation, not physical deployment claims

This package does not present `hcbm-1` as a validated direct-air-capture
material.

## 7. Public Non-Goals

This package does not publish:

- internal discovery heuristics
- internal scoring weights
- internal rejected candidates
- internal deployment models
- internal product or governance mechanics

## 8. Public Next Step

The honest next step after this package is external experimental or independent
simulation work by the community.

Until that happens, `hcbm-1` should be treated as an openly published material
candidate, not as a proven material.
